Overview

Today, the vast majority of the wars and conflicts around the world are not fought between state militaries, but instead between non state actors and state militaries. These non state actors come in all shapes and sizes, with some being local cell terrorist groups and others being multinational terrorist enterprises. Some groups are supported discreetly or publicly by state actors, while others gun it alone and are universally hated. Some groups confess the purity of the white race, while others proclaim that the only difference between an Arab and a non-Arab is their Iman, or piety in Islam. Regardless of who they are, if they fester in the deserts of the Sahara or the urban landscapes of the midwest, this website is dedicated to tracking them, their history, ideology, tactics, and notable actions.

My goal is to expand this site to include every non state actor or paramilitary group in the world. I also aim to create a blog of news and analysis of topics and modern history western media barely mentions at all.
