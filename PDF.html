The People's Democratic Forces

Updated: 2/9/25

Overview

The People's Democratic Forces (PDF) is the armed wing of the National Unity Government (NUG) in Myanmar. The NUG was established after the Tatmadaw's military coup in 2021-an alternative to the Junta controlled State Administrative Council (SAC). The PDF aligned with ethnic armed organizations (EAOs) and waged guerilla warfare against the Tatmadaw regime. The Burmese opposition would be injected new energy in 2023, when the PDF and aligned EAOs launched Operation 1027, coming to occupy several towns and cities. Operation 1027 inspired a nationwide campaign against the Tatmadaw, drawing other EAOs from the rest of Myanmar. The PDF believes in a form of federalism, democracy, and autonomy for Burmese minority groups.
