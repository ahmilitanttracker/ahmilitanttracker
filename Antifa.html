Antifa

Updated: 2/9/25

Overview

Antifa, sometimes called Antifascist Action, is a movement and term for American antifascist and leftist protesters who use violent militant black bloc tactics. This label for protesters was popularized during the first Trump presidency and has grown ever since. Protesters who have used the Antifa label have used clubs, dried liquids, and doxing against far-right neo-nazi activists and events like Charlottesville in 2017. The Antifa "movement" was implicated in the murder of a far-right counter protester during the George Floyd protests, resulting in the first act of murder by any American anti-fascists in over 25 years. Antifa in the United States was designated as a "domestic terrorist organization", despite that title not having any water and despite "Antifa" not being a centralized group. In November of 2025, "Antifa Ost", a left wing German direct action group, was designated as a foreign terrorist organization.

Ideology & Tactics

The Antifa movement represents the most violent edge of left-wingers and socialists in the United States . Due to its decentralized and leaderless nature, Antifa "member's" only distinct ideology is militant anti-fascism. Due to this, the Antifa movement includes anarchists, communists, socialists, anti-capitalists, and other varieties of left wing thought. Many members are part of or support protection of racial, ethnic, and sexual minorities. Tactically, the Antifa movement use "black bloc", which is masking their identity and wearing all black, during attacks and counter-protests against neo-nazis and neo-fascists at large political gatherings. On the Internet, Antifa supporters organize through social media, encrypted networks and encrypted messaging platforms. In addition, supporters infiltrate, dox, monitor, and research fascist and neo-nazi groups.

Notable Actions

• August 12, 2017: Antifa as a movement would make its name known to the nation when members engaged in violent skirmishes against white nationalists during the Unite the Right rally. Members used clubs and dyed liquids to attack far-right protesters, resulting in a dozen injured white nationalists. In the end, one antifascist counterprotester was killed and 35 were injured by a car ramming done by neo-nazis. Later, president Trump said that there were good people on both sides and asked the press about the violent actions of the "alt left".

• August 27, 2017: Clashes started on Berkeley campus between Antifa and right wingers when the right wing "Say No to Marxism" rally was planned. During clashes, Antifa was able to overpower a police perimeter. In the months preceding it, various right wing influencers were invited to speak in Berkeley, sparking intense violence and protests. In one instance in February, 150 Antifa members broke into Berkeley and caused $100,000 worth of damage.

• August 31, 2017: During the aftermath of the devastating Hurricane Harvey, members of the Antifa movement involved themselves in aid, rescue, and recovery efforts. Perhaps they were taking a note from the Black Panthers of the 70s, an American black nationalist militant organization that also participated in community relief efforts.

• November 8, 2018: A group of Antifa activists visited the personal house of popular far-right FOX news political commentator Tucker Carlson in Washington D.C.. The members of Smash Racism D.C., an Antifa associated group, shouted epithets, including threats to him and his family, tore down his front door, and sprayed the symbol of anarchy on his driveway. The group was later banned from Twitter for doxing Carlson by posting the address of his house.

• July 13, 2019: During anti-ICE protests in Tacoma, Washington, Willem Van Spronsen, an Antifa sympathizer and long time member of the Puget Sound John Brown Gun Club, was shot and killed by local officers after lunging molotov cocktails at buildings and vehicles. The attack was soon believed to be a suicide attempt conducted in an ideologically sound way.

• June 8, 2020: During the George Floyd protests, protesters moved into the Capitol Hill area of Seattle, Washington state, and declared it "Free Capitol Hill". The Mayor respected the decision and witheld police from Capitol Hill for the time being. While much of the action was peaceful protest, coop building, and community giving, Antifa and other violent agitators took advantage of the lack of police to loot stores and cause damage. By the zone's abolishment and the reoccupation by police, two teenagers were dead in "Free Capitol Hill".

• August 29, 2020: In a George Floyd Protest in Portland, Oregon, right wing protester and Patriot Prayer member Aaron Danielson was shot and killed by left wing Antifa inspired and supporter Micheal Reinoehl. In an video acquired by VICE, Reinoehl tried to justify the killing by saying that the Patriot Prayer member was threatening to stab a friend of color next to him. Later, while on the run, Micheal Reinoehl was shot and killed in a confrontation with police in Lacey, Washington, an event that made Donald Trump ecstatic.

• March 5, 2023: More than 20 agitators in black masks lunged rocks and firebombs at the construction site of the "Cop City" police training facility. They were later arrested on charges of domestic terrorism. The Stop Cop City protests had resulted in the death of an eco-anarchist by the hands of the police.

• February 26, 2024: An antifascist detonated an IED outside of the office of Alabama's highly controversial attorney general (who shot down IVF). Kyle Benjamin D. Calvert, the perpetrator of said crime, had placed stickers that advocated left wing positions on a number of state buildings, including one that said "support your local Antifa ".

• July 4, 2025: A group of 11 left wing militants arrived at an ICE detention facility in Alvarado, Texas, and started to vandalize ICE vehicles and fire fireworks at the building. Another suspect had allegedly fired 20 to 30 rounds at the building, injuring one local police officer. Law enforcement later found tactical gear including AR-15 rifles and flags depicting left wing ideology. The United States Department of Justice claimed that the attack was carried out by "Antifa ". A few months later, Antifa was designated as a "domestic terrorist organization".
