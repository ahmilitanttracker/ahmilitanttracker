The Atomwaffen Division

Updated: 2/9/25

Overview

The Atomwaffen Division (AWD), later branded as the National Socialist Resistance Front, is a mostly dormant international neo-nazi accelerationist terrorist network based in the United States . Since its foundation in 2013, the group has had members held responsible for murders, bombings, and planned terrorist attacks. It is one of the few neo-nazi groups to have a truly international grasp, having offshoots in multiple countries including the United Kingdom (as the Sonnenkrieg Division), Germany , Canada , the Baltic States (as the Feuerkrieg Division), Russia , Italy , Finland , France , Argentina , Brazil , and Spain . The group advocates and plans violent terrorist attacks against the United States government and for accelerationism, an ideology that promotes terrorism to speed up the collapse of modern society and the rise of a white nationalist one. Following James Mason's announcement of the American branches' apparent disbanding, AWD splintered into a variety of satanist, neo-nazi , and even "pro-rape" groups. Due to its violent methods, AWD and its international offshoots have been listed as terrorist groups by the United Kingdom , Canada , Australia , Austria , Italy , and Estonia .

Ideology & Tactics

The Atomwaffen Division and its various international affiliates follow an ideology of militant accelerationist neo-nazism. Every American AWD member in training was required to read SIEGE by James Mason, the ideologue and spokesperson for the group. In Siege, neo-nazis are recommended to launch lone wolf terrorist attacks instead of mass organizing like other neo-nazi groups. The book advocates for creating pure white ethnostates by starting race wars through random acts of terrorism. Uniquely, Mason also promotes and takes inspiration from cult leader Charles Manson, supporting his idea of "helter skelter". Siege attracts young men by saying that society is at fault for them not fitting in. Mason states that outcasts should wear it as a "badge of honor". This statement has helped propagate the promotion of misogyny and sexual assault. To train members who carry out attacks like the ones described in Siege, the AWD seeks former military personnel who can spread their knowledge in explosives, firearms training, and insurgency within the group. Lastly, like jihadist or islamist militant groups, the AWD release propaganda videos of their paramilitary training. In said videos, "hate camps", or buildings where members conduct militaristic training, are shown. In the propaganda releases, in order to visually show their message in a way similar to the nazis of old, members burn the flags of their perceived enemies, including the US , the UN, the LGBT movement, Israel etc. To attract new members, the AWD uses propaganda posters and online forums like the neo-nazi Iron March and encrypted groups on "terrorgram". After the arrest of Brandon Russell, the group's founder, John Cameron Denton (also known as "Rape") assumed leadership, moving the group into a satanic direction, allying with the Order of Nine Angels and the Tower ov Blood. The O9A have also infiltrated other branches of the Atomwaffen Division, including the Sonnenkrieg Division and the Feuerkrieg Division. Despite its "disbanding" by James Mason, the AWD continue to inspire copycat shootings and bombings, resulting in the deaths of dozens.

Notable Actions

Fig 1. ASSEMBLY OF IEDS AND EXPLOSIVES IN THE HOME OF BRANDON RUSSELL, WHO WAS PREPARING FOR ATTACKS ON SUBSTATIONS c. 2017-2018

• May 19, 2017: Devon Arthurs, an Atomwaffen Division member who had converted to radical islam, killed two of his roommates in their abode in Tampa Florida for disapproving of his faith, before leaving to hold 3 people hostage at gunpoint and eventually surrendering to local police. Authorities found chemicals, explosive materials, and posters supporting violent neo-nazi ideology. In police custody, Arthurs confessed that the group was very dangerous and seeking to target facilities. Later that day, Brandon Russell, the founder of the group and roommate of Arthurs, was arrested on an explosives charge in a store near a chemical plant with body armor, ammunition, and firearms.

• August 12, 2017: Greco-American AWD member and USMC corporal Vasillios Pistolis was recorded in Unite the Right’s torch bearing rally and was later found beating up left wing counterprotesters with a neo-nazi flag. He was court martialed and kicked out of active duty USMC a year later.

• December 22, 2017: AWD member Nicholas Giampa shot and killed his girlfriend’s parents in their home in Reston, Virginia. The girlfriend’s parents were highly disapproving of Giampa’s neo–nazi ideology, leading to an altercation that resulted in the young AWD member killing them both before shooting and injuring himself. Giampa became lionized by the AWD and their posters.

• January 2, 2018: In Lake Forest, Southern California, Gay Jewish university student Blaze Bernstein was murdered by AWD member and peer Sam Woodward. He was lured into a park for a “date”, pulling him in closer before killing him by stabbing. When the story was revealed to the public, Woodward was called a “one man gay Jew wrecking crew” by his AWD peers.

• June 1, 2018: A video was released announcing the foundation of Atomwaffen Deutschland , the group's German affiliate. In the video, they promised action and a "long fight". In the coming years, AWD-D members would be arrested for attempting or planning various terrorist attacks. AWD-D material was acquired by Stephen Balliet, who later killed two people while trying to force himself into a synagogue in the state of Saxony-Anhalt.

• September 23, 2019: Army Specialist and AWD member Jarrett William Smith was arrested and charged with distributing bomb making materials over social media sources. He was planning to join the Azov Battalion and be with his fellow AWD member, mentor, and Azov foreign fighter Craig Lang. Lang was on the run for killing two people in Florida during an armed robbery before he was extradited to the US in 2024.

Fig 2. STILL OF A FKD VIDEO CLAIMING RESPONSIBILITY FOR A BOMBING IN A WESTERN UNION IN LITHUANIA c. OCTOBER OF 2019

• October 8, 2019: The Feuerkrieg Division, the Baltic affiliate of AWD , made their presence known to the wider region by claiming responsibility for a placed but unexploded IED near a Western Union in Lithuania through video. In said video, they flashed headlines of the incident and directly said that their threats aren't empty. A year later, a 21 year old Lithuanian man was charged for the bombing with many firearms and explosives found at his house.

• December 19, 2019: A Russian neo-nazi affiliated with the organization Ethnic National Union opened fire on the FSB HQ in Moscow, killing two and injuring 5 before he himself was killed. His apartment was covered with posters and propaganda from AWD's Russian branch Atomwaffen Division Russland . Perhaps trying to pin the blame on muslims and muslim migrants in Russia , he exclaimed slogans used by ISIS.

• February 6, 2020: The FBI arrested 5 senior AWD leaders across multiple states following evidence coming out that AWD was planning to terrorize, threaten, and intimidate jewish journalists and journalists of color. This operation was called “Operation Erste Saule”, translated to Operation First Pillar.

• March 14, 2020: James Mason, the ideologue of AWD , went on video to announce the group's disbanding. He justified the decision saying that the FBI and their infiltrations had made deep cuts in the organization, deep enough that the organization was too weak to continue. This move came after much speculation that AWD would soon be listed as a terrorist group by the American Government (which did not happen in the end). However, analysts believed that this move was primarily done to give members some breathing room to recuperate and regroup. Fatalities and activities in the United States decreased as the group splintered into other groups.

• October 8, 2020: Two American AWD members who sought to join the Azov Battalion , a group with alleged neo-nazi sympathies, were arrested and deported from Ukraine . The members sought to gain military experience from Azov , which would allegedly be used for illegal activities. In Ukraine , they filmed videos calling for white Americans to do terrorism.

• December 4, 2021: 5 members of an Atomwaffen Division Finland cell were arrested on suspicion of planning to commit a terror attack against a refugee center. Police confiscated numerous firearms, 40kg worth of explosives, and hundreds of liters of material for explosives. The arrests came after 2 or so years of wiretapping and surveillance by the police. AWD Finland is known to be affiliated with O9A satanic ideology.

• February 6, 2023: After coming out of jail, Brandon Russell was arrested again with his girlfriend at the time for conspiracy to commit an act of terror against energy facilities in an attempt to “completely destroy the city”. His plan included maps, possible months for execution, and possible substations to hit. He was found guilty for his charge 2 years later.

• August 5, 2024: Members of the British wing of the AWD , the Sonnenkrieg Division, planned and were a part of many of the riots that occurred in the United Kingdom following the stabbing and murder of three children by a supposed muslim migrant. But in fact, the stabbing was done by a black British teenager born in Wales.

• October 30, 2024: A member of the AWD branch in Canada , the Northern Order, hacked into 160 companies and held their information ransom. Apparently, At&T paid $370,000 to the hacker.

• December 4, 2024: The houses of members of the splinter cell of Atomwaffen in Italy , the Wehrwolf Division, were raided and members arrested on charges of conspiracy to kill the Prime Minister and possession of illegal firearms. Interestingly, this neo-nazi terror cell was apparently connected to jihadists in Palestine . One member of the group had previously fought alongside the militant group Lions Den, while another member was a Palestinian who built explosives for the Wehrwolf Division.

• November 20, 2025: Law Enforcement carried out a search warrant on a 20 year old in Sarasota, southern Florida, on count of illegal firearms possession. He was also suspected to have planned out possible acts of violence with fire arms and an IED. They found neo-nazi and violent nihilist material, including the flag of the Atomwaffen Division . He was later indicted for the earlier charge and a new one for CSAM.
